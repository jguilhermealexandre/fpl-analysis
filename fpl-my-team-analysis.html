How to use this page
👆
Swap two players
Click a player on the pitch, then click another in the same position. Valid targets glow while you're choosing; click the first player again to cancel.
🅲
Set captain & vice
Use the small C and V buttons above each player. The captain's projected score is doubled.
✨
Auto-Optimize
Builds the highest-projected valid lineup for you — always a legal formation, never just the top 11 scorers.
⚖️
AI Scouting Report
Tick the checkbox on any two or more players — in the squad table or a transfer panel — and an action bar appears at the bottom to compare them.
📅
Fixture calendar
Click the fixture chips on any squad row to see that club's next 10 gameweeks, including blanks and doubles.
