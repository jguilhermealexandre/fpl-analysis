Making a report useful

If you are reporting something wrong, these details turn "it's broken" into something fixable in one pass:

  • Which page, and which section of it.
  • What you expected versus what you saw. For a number that looks wrong, the specific player and the specific figure is ideal.
  • Your Team ID, if the issue involves your own squad. It is public, and it lets us reproduce exactly what you are seeing.
  • Device and browser, if it is a layout or loading problem.
  • A screenshot, which is usually worth all of the above combined.

Your Fantasy Premier League password or login details. EasyFPL never needs them, and nobody legitimate will ever ask you for them — not us, and not anyone claiming to be us.

Quick answers

A few things come up often enough to answer here.

Where do I find my Team ID?

Log in to the official Fantasy Premier League site, open the "Points" or "My Team" tab, and look at the URL. It contains /entry/-------/, and the digits in place of those dashes are your Team ID. It is public information, and it is all EasyFPL needs.

Is my data safe?

There is no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Using the site needs no account: your Team ID and your settings live in your own browser and are never sent to us. If you do create an optional account, it stores your email address, your plan and your Team ID, and it sets two cookies to keep you signed in — nothing else. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Is EasyFPL free?

Every page is free to everyone today — billing is not switched on. There is a published Premium plan at £3 a month, and the pricing page says exactly which pages it will cover when it starts. Nothing on this site has ever required an account.

About the project

EasyFPL is an independent, free tool built by a fantasy manager who wanted better answers than a spreadsheet could give. It runs on the official Fantasy Premier League API, does its analysis in your browser, and tries very hard not to over-claim — including telling you plainly when the data is too thin to support a confident answer.
