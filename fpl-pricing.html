Before you ask

The money questions

Is the free tier crippled?

No, and the table above is the whole answer. Free is the read — the dashboard, the Desk, the news, the price moves — and it is enough to run a season on. Premium is the four screens that do the deciding.

Do I need an account?

Not to use the site. Your public FPL Team ID is the way in, and it is not a password — it identifies a page anyone can already see. An account only becomes necessary when there is something to bill, and even then it holds an email address, a plan and that same Team ID.

Where does the data come from?

The official Fantasy Premier League API, rebuilt every four hours and read live during matches. No scraping, no private feed, nothing typed in by hand.

Can I cancel?

Whenever you like, once billing exists. No contract and no minimum term. Nothing on the free side of the table moves to the other side later — that is what publishing the split this early is for.
