Before you ask

The money questions

Is the free tier crippled?

No. Every analysis page is free, including the Transfer Wizard and the rivals board. Premium is about alerts, history and saving work — not about seeing the numbers.

Do I need an account?

Not for the free tier. Your public FPL Team ID is the way in, and it is not a password — it identifies a page anyone can already see.

Where does the data come from?

The official Fantasy Premier League API, refreshed through the gameweek. Nothing here is scraped from a third party or typed in by hand.

Can I cancel?

Whenever you like, once billing exists. There is no contract and no minimum term, and the free tier stays exactly as it is.
