What you can actually ask it

Ranked, with a reason

Every recommendation carries a written verdict — the xGI per ninety, the difficulty of the run, whether the minutes are secure — so you can disagree with it for a stated reason rather than a vibe.

Filters that matter

Position, price ceiling, form window, ownership band and fixture difficulty. Combine them to answer a real question: a £6m midfielder under 10% owned with three easy fixtures.

Compare side by side

Put any two players next to each other on expected goals, assists, minutes, bonus and projected points. The differences that decide it are usually not the headline ones.

Differentials, defined properly

Ownership is shown against the field, so a player who is 4% owned globally but in half your mini-league is correctly not a differential for you.

Worth knowing

Three habits it is built to break

Buying last week

Points already scored are the worst predictor on the page. The projection looks forward; the form column is context, not the answer.

Trusting a hot streak

Finishing is noisy over ten matches and expected goals is not. The model leans on the chances, not the conversions.

Ignoring the bench risk

A brilliant player at 60% start probability is a worse pick than a steady one at 95%, and the ranking already knows that.
