Metrics & Terminology
xG stands for Expected Goals — a measure of attacking threat based on the quality of chances created. xGA stands for Expected Goals Against — a measure of defensive vulnerability. We use these metrics to produce “Traffic Light” verdicts on whether a fixture is favorable or tough for your players.
FDR is the Fixture Difficulty Rating, on a scale of 1 (easiest) to 5 (hardest). It’s used throughout the site to color-code fixtures. Green (1–2) means favorable fixtures where your players are more likely to return, while red (4–5) indicates tough matchups. Use FDR to plan transfers and captaincy picks over multiple gameweeks.
The Captain Score is a custom metric that combines a player’s recent form (Points per Game over the last 5 matches) with the FDR of their upcoming opponent to project their explosive potential. Higher scores indicate players who are both in form and facing favorable opponents.
Star players are in top form with great upcoming fixtures — keep and consider captaining. Hold players are performing adequately or have improving fixtures ahead. Sell players have poor form, tough fixtures, or injury concerns and should be prioritized for transfer out.
How the Numbers Work
Every projected-points figure on the site comes from one model, built from the actual FPL scoring rules rather than fitted as a black box. It adds up appearance points, goals and assists from expected goals and expected assists, clean sheets, saves, defensive contribution and bonus, then subtracts goals conceded and cards — each term scaled by how likely the player is to start and how long he is likely to last. The methodology page breaks down every term with the formulas.

Form is average points over the last 30 days, so one gameweek into a season it is that one gameweek — and on that basis 52% of every player who got on the pitch counted as “out of form”, which is really just “did not score three points once”. Form is now regressed toward each player’s own points per game from last season, reaching the raw figure at four gameweeks. See how squad health is built.
