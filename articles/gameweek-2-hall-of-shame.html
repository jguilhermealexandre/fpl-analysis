Gameweek 2 Hall of Shame: the blanks, the cameos, and the captain who cost you

The awards nobody wants from Gameweek 2, starting with Thiago. Every one of them a real number, which is the problem.

4 September 2026 · Official FPL data

The awards nobody wants

Every gameweek produces a handful of moments that are only funny once they cannot hurt you any more. Gameweek 2 has finished, so here they are.

Every award below is a real lookup against the round's data. Nothing here is invented, which is the part that stings.

The short version
  • Thiago takes the Golden Blank at £8.0m.
  • The field captained Haaland for 13 points.
  • Scott scored 12 at 2% ownership.

🪦 The Golden Blank

£8.0m buys a lot of things. On this occasion it bought 90 minutes of Thiago and 2 points, which 13.6% of you had budgeted for rather differently.

🧢 The Armband Tax

Haaland was the popular armband and returned 13 points, which the armband turned into 26. A rare week where the crowd and the scoreboard agreed.

🃏 The One You Did Not Own

12 points from Scott, owned by 2%. Somewhere a very small number of people are having their best Saturday of the season and telling absolutely everybody.

⏱️ Cameo of the Week

18 minutes. 4 points. Diarra did more after coming on than several people did all afternoon, most of them in your starting eleven.

💰 Best Value on the Board

Dedić, £4.5m, 11 points, 2.4 per million. Quietly the most sensible thing that happened all weekend.

🎯 Mugged by the Woodwork

Barry generated 1.48 expected goal involvements and converted precisely none of them. The underlying numbers liked him a great deal more than the scoreboard did, which is either a buy signal or a warning depending on how your season is going.

For the record

The average manager scored 81, the best score in the game was 161, and the gap between them was 80 points — roughly a captain, a bench and a small amount of luck.

Scout's key takeaway

None of this is advice. The advice is next door, and it is considerably better behaved.
