Gameweek 5 Hall of Shame: the blanks, the cameos, and the captain who cost you

The awards nobody wants from Gameweek 5, starting with B.Fernandes. Every one of them a real number, which is the problem.

21 September 2026 · Official FPL data

The awards nobody wants

Gameweek 5 is in the books, the damage is priced in, and it is now safe to laugh about it.

Every award below is a real lookup against the round's data. Nothing here is invented, which is the part that stings.

The short version
  • B.Fernandes takes the Golden Blank at £12.0m.
  • The field captained Haaland for 6 points.
  • Dasilva scored 15 at 0.7% ownership.

The Golden Blank

B.Fernandes, £12.0m, 90 minutes, 2 points. He was owned by 38.9% of the game going in, so at least the disappointment was catered.

The Armband Tax

The field captained Haaland and, for once, the field was right: 6 became 12. Enjoy it. This award is not usually a compliment.

The One You Did Not Own

Dasilva (£4.0m) scored 15 while owned by 0.7% of managers. The other 99.3% are currently explaining to themselves that they would never have started him, which is true, and beside the point.

Cameo of the Week

Carvalho was on the pitch for 3 minutes and came off it with 6 points. Efficiency of that order should be studied, or at least owned.

Best Value on the Board

Dasilva at £4.0m returned 15 — 3.8 points per million. Premium ownership is a lifestyle choice and this is the invoice.

Mugged by the Woodwork

0.97 xGI, zero returns: Gonzalo did everything but the last bit. Historically the last bit arrives. Historically it arrives the week after you sell him.

For the record

The average manager scored 48, the best score in the game was 126, and the gap between them was 78 points — roughly a captain, a bench and a small amount of luck.

Scout's key takeaway

The useful version of this gameweek is in the Debrief. This one was for morale.
