The gap between chances and goals

Expected goal involvements measure the chances a player gets into. Goals and assists measure what came of them. When the first is well ahead of the second, one of two things is true: the player has been unlucky, or he is not good enough to finish what he creates. Over a long enough run it is almost always the first.
