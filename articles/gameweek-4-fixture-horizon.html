The next six weeks

FPL is played in blocks, not in single weeks. The teams worth buying are rarely the ones with the best fixture on Saturday — they are the ones whose schedule turns over the next month, bought before the turn rather than after it. This is the run from Gameweek 5 to Gameweek 10.

Schedule density

Every team plays exactly 6 fixtures across this window — no doubles, no blanks. The schedule is even, so the fixture question here is purely about difficulty rather than volume.

Home and away

The same six fixtures are worth materially different amounts depending on where they are played. Home advantage in the Premier League is worth somewhere around a fifth of a goal a game — small in isolation, and the difference between a good run and an ordinary one across a block.

What a swing is worth by position

A fixture swing does not pay every position equally, which is why "buy the good fixtures" is incomplete advice.

  • Defenders and goalkeepers gain the most. Clean sheets are close to binary and heavily fixture-dependent: a defender facing the weakest attacks in the division is a different asset to the same defender facing the strongest, and the four points arrive all at once.
  • Midfielders gain least from easy fixtures and lose least from hard ones. Attacking returns are driven more by the player's own involvement than by the opponent, and the good ones create chances against anybody.
  • Forwards sit in between, with one caveat: a poor team's forward in a good run is still a poor team's forward. Fixture swings amplify a team's attacking quality rather than substituting for it.

The practical version is that a defensive asset is worth buying into a swing and selling out of it, while a genuine premium attacker is usually worth holding through a hard run rather than transferring twice.

Risk check

Fixture difficulty is FPL's own rating, set before the season and only lightly revised. It knows nothing about injuries, form, or a manager change, and it rates the badge rather than the team currently wearing it. Across a six-week block it is directionally right more often than not, which is the most that can be claimed for it.
