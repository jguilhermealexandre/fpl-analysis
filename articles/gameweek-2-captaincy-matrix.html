The armband is the biggest call you make

⚠️ Risk check

Effective ownership — the figure that actually decides whether a captaincy gains you rank — depends on how many managers captain a player, not just how many own him. FPL does not publish that number, and nothing on this page estimates it. The ownership column above is ownership. Treat the gap between the two as unknown rather than small.

What the field did last time

The most-captained player in Gameweek 1 was Haaland, and he returned 2 — 4 with the armband on.

The best available return was De Cuyper on 17, so the difference between the crowd's armband and the optimal one was 30 points. Chasing the optimal captain every week is not a strategy — nobody hits it — but the size of that gap is why the decision deserves more thought than it usually gets.

The vice-captaincy

The vice only pays out when your captain does not play, which makes it a decision most managers make carelessly and then regret twice a season. The rule that matters: your vice should kick off after your captain wherever possible, so that a captain benched at the last minute can still be covered — and he should never be a rotation risk himself, because a captain who does not play backed by a vice who also does not play is a guaranteed zero on a doubled slot.

Ndiaye (16% owned, away at BOU) is the natural cover here.

Three ways this goes wrong

  • Captaining form into a wall. A player in good touch against a strong defence away from home is a worse bet than an average player at home to a poor one. Form is a weaker signal than the fixture, and it is the one that feels more compelling.
  • Chasing last week. The player who returned fifteen last gameweek is not more likely to return this one, but he is more likely to be captained. That is the mechanism by which the field collectively buys high.
  • Going differential for its own sake. An off-template armband should be justified by the fixture, not by the ownership. If the only argument for a captain is that few people own him, that is an argument for a lottery ticket.
