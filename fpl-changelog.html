21 Sep 2026

A new landing page, and pages that were missing

  • Rebuilt the landing page around screenshots of the real product rather than drawings of it.

20 Sep 2026

Rivals, and a link that points rather than covers

  • Squad Strength puts the pitch beside its read-outs instead of above them.
  • Captain Race reads in two columns, with portraits in the tables.
  • A link from the dashboard now scrolls to the player's row and pulses it, rather than opening a card over the page.
  • Rising Form ranks the player rather than his club's fixture list.
19 Sep 2026

Transfer Wizard: the cap is the plan's

  • Single caps at one transfer, Multi at five, and a chip at fifteen — the counter used to allow five under every plan.
  • "5 / 1 free" now reads as what it is: five staged, one of them free, the rest at −4.
  • Fixed a second goalkeeper appearing on the overview pitch.
  • The odds calibration had silently been measuring nothing since the xG engine moved file.
