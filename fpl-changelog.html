What changed, and when

Changes to the analysis tools, a week at a time — what is new, and what was wrong and now is not. Data refreshes run several times a day and are not listed here.

Week of 15 SepGW5 – GW6

Rivals gets readable, and the Transfer Wizard stops over-promising

fix

The Transfer Wizard’s transfer cap is now the plan’s own: one under Single, five under Multi, fifteen on a chip. Single was letting you stage five, which is the opposite of what it says it does.

fix

The transfers counter read "5 / 1 free", which parses as five allowed, one free. It now reads as what it is — five staged, one of them free, the rest at −4.

fix

A second goalkeeper could appear on the wizard’s overview pitch. The arrangement is validated before it draws.

new

Squad Strength puts the pitch beside its read-outs instead of above them, and the Captain Race reads in two columns.

new

Players have faces in the rivals tables, and Your Differentials are player cards rather than bare rows.

fix

Clicking a player in "Needs a look" now scrolls to their row and pulses it rather than opening a card over the page you were sent to.

Week of 8 SepGW3 – GW4

The Scout’s Desk learns to rank the player, not the fixture

fix

Rising Form was ranking a player by his club’s fixture list rather than by his own underlying numbers turning. It ranks the player now.

fix

One team-xG model across the site. Two existed, and they disagreed by up to 6% a club because one summed season totals where the other summed per-gameweek rows.

new

The price ticker reads the room — it shows what is close to changing tonight rather than a loop of everything.

new

Signals on the desk name their icon, and the board is archived each week rather than overwritten.

Week of 1 SepGW1 – GW2

Marking the homework

new

Every projection is now sealed at the deadline and graded against what actually happened once the round is final. The results feed the accuracy page.

new

The model is scored against FPL’s own ep_next and against points-per-game, so the number has something to beat.

fix

A wildcard week now correctly freezes the free-transfer bank instead of spending from it.

fix

The deadline card ends at the countdown; the attention grid that repeated the readiness bar underneath it is gone.

Late AugustPre-season

Getting in, and three ways to spend a transfer

new

Your Team ID is typed straight into the header bar rather than behind a button that opens a box to type it in.

new

A dedicated page for linking a squad, and three suggested ways to spend a transfer when you open the wizard.

fix

Lazy images that never loaded until something looked at them — fixed across the player lists.
