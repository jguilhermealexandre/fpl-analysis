Summary

EasyFPL is a static website. The analysis runs in your browser, not on a server we control. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not ask for your email address to use the tools, and we never ask for your Fantasy Premier League password — the site only ever uses your public Team ID.

In one line

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data. Your Team ID, your draft plans and your settings live in your own browser's local storage and stay there.

What we collect

Nothing. EasyFPL operates no user database, no account system and no analytics platform. There is no server-side log of who you are or what you looked at that we can read.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses or contact details — unless you choose to email us, in which case we have your email because you sent it
  • Fantasy Premier League passwords or login credentials, ever, for any reason
  • Payment details — the site is free and takes no payments
  • Behavioural or advertising profiles

When you enter a Team ID, your browser requests that team's public data directly from the official Fantasy Premier League API. That request passes through a lightweight proxy we run, which exists solely because browsers block direct cross-origin requests to the FPL API. The proxy forwards the request, briefly caches the public response to reduce load on the official API, and does not build any profile of who asked for what.

What your browser stores

To avoid making you retype things, the site saves a small amount of data in your browser's local storage. This is not a cookie, it is never transmitted to us, and you can clear it at any time.

Stored itemWhy
Team IDSo you do not re-enter it on every visit
Mini-league IDSame, for the League Rivals tool
Draft plans & notesYour multi-gameweek plans and any notes you write against them
ShortlistPlayers you have flagged to watch
Analysis settingsYour chosen presets, weightings and sensitivity
Interface preferencesTheme, sidebar state, which panels you left open

To erase all of it, clear site data for easyfpl.com in your browser settings, or use a private browsing window. The site will simply ask for your Team ID again next time.

EasyFPL also registers a service worker, which caches pages and assets on your device so the site loads quickly and keeps working on a poor connection. This is a local cache. It stores no personal information and sends nothing anywhere.

Cookies and tracking

EasyFPL sets no cookies. There is no analytics script, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no session tracking and no fingerprinting on this site. This is not a policy promise we ask you to take on trust — it is a property of the code, which contains no such integrations.

Because we set no cookies and run no tracking, there is no consent banner. There is nothing to consent to.

Children

EasyFPL is a football statistics tool intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no personal data from anyone, we hold no data about children either.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data an organisation holds about you. We hold none, so in practice:

  • Access and export. Everything is already on your device, in your browser's local storage.
  • Deletion. Clearing site data for easyfpl.com deletes it completely and immediately. You do not need to ask us.
  • Objection. There is no processing to object to, because there is no processing.

If you email us, we hold that email as long as needed to reply to you. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Changes to this policy

If the site ever adds something that changes the picture above — analytics, accounts, or anything that collects personal data — this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date at the top will say when. We will not quietly start collecting data and update the policy afterwards.
