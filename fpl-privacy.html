Privacy Policy

Summary

EasyFPL is a static website. The analysis runs in your browser, not on a server we control. Using the site requires no account and no email address, and we never ask for your Fantasy Premier League password — the tools only ever need your public Team ID.

There is one exception, and it is opt-in: you may create an account. If you do, that account has an email address and a password, and both are held by our authentication provider. Everything below distinguishes between the two cases, because they are genuinely different.

In one line

Without an account nothing about you reaches us at all: your Team ID, your drafts and your settings live in your own browser and stay there. With an account we hold your email address, your plan and your Team ID, and nothing else.

What we collect

If you do not make an account: nothing. There is no analytics platform and no server-side log of who you are or what you looked at that we can read. Every tool on this site works in that state.

If you do make an account, we hold exactly three things about you, and you can see all three:

HeldWhy
Your email addressIt is how you sign back in, and how a password is reset
Your planFree or Premium, and the date it runs to
Your FPL Team IDSo a second device knows your squad without you retyping the number

That is the whole record. It is stored with Supabase, our authentication provider, and it is protected by row-level security so that an account can read its own row and no other.

In neither case do we collect:

  • Fantasy Premier League passwords or login credentials, ever, for any reason
  • Payment details — billing is not switched on, and when it is, card details will go to the payment processor and never to us
  • Behavioural or advertising profiles
  • Any record of which pages you opened or which players you looked at

When you enter a Team ID, your browser requests that team's public data directly from the official Fantasy Premier League API. That request passes through a lightweight proxy we run, which exists solely because browsers block direct cross-origin requests to the FPL API. The proxy forwards the request, briefly caches the public response to reduce load on the official API, and does not build any profile of who asked for what.

What your browser stores

To avoid making you retype things, the site saves a small amount of data in your browser's local storage. This is not a cookie, it is never transmitted to us, and you can clear it at any time.

To erase all of it, clear site data for easyfpl.com in your browser settings, or use a private browsing window. The site will simply ask for your Team ID again next time.

EasyFPL also registers a service worker, which caches pages and assets on your device so the site loads quickly and keeps working on a poor connection. This is a local cache. It stores no personal information and sends nothing anywhere.

Cookies and tracking

There is no tracking of any kind. No analytics script, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no fingerprinting. This is not a promise we ask you to take on trust — it is a property of the code, which contains no such integrations, and you can confirm it in the network tab of any browser.

Two cookies exist, and only while you are signed in to an account. If you never make one, this site sets no cookies at all.

CookieWhat it isHow long
easyfpl_atThe access token that keeps you signed inUntil it expires, typically an hour
easyfpl_rtThe refresh token that renews the one above without asking you to sign in again30 days

Both are set Secure and SameSite=Lax, both are readable only by this site, and signing out deletes both. They carry a session and nothing else: no identifier that follows you to another site, and nothing that describes what you did here.

Why there is no cookie banner

Under the UK and EU rules, consent is required for cookies that are not strictly necessary — analytics, advertising, and anything that profiles you. It is not required for a cookie whose only job is to keep you signed in to a service you asked to sign in to. Those two are the only cookies here, and there is no analytics or advertising of any kind, so there is nothing a banner could ask you about. If that ever changes, a banner will appear before the change ships, and the date at the top of this page will say when.

Children

EasyFPL is a football statistics tool intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no personal data from anyone, we hold no data about children either.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data an organisation holds about you. We hold none, so in practice:

  • Access and export. Everything is already on your device, in your browser's local storage.
  • Deletion. Clearing site data for easyfpl.com deletes it completely and immediately. You do not need to ask us.
  • Objection. There is no processing to object to, because there is no processing.

If you email us, we hold that email as long as needed to reply to you. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Changes to this policy

If the site ever adds something that changes the picture above — analytics, accounts, or anything that collects personal data — this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date at the top will say when. We will not quietly start collecting data and update the policy afterwards.
